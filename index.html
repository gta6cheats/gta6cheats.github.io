How cheats worked in past GTA games

Every main GTA game from the 1997 original to GTA V had cheats. On consoles you typed a button sequence during play. GTA V added phone-number cheats you dialled on the in-game phone.

In GTA V, using a cheat turned off trophies and achievements for that session, and cheats never worked in GTA Online. We'll find out after launch whether GTA 6 does the same, and update this page.

GTA 6 cheats for PS5

On PS5, GTA cheats have always been button sequences using ✕ ○ △ □, L1, R1, L2, R2 and the d-pad, entered during play. Pick PS5 at the top of this page and every code shows in PlayStation buttons.

GTA 6 cheats for Xbox Series X|S

On Xbox, the same cheats use A, B, X, Y, LB, RB, LT, RT and the d-pad. Pick Xbox at the top and the buttons switch over.

GTA 6 phone cheat numbers

GTA V let you dial cheats on the in-game phone, and they worked the same on every console. Phone numbers for GTA 6 go under the Phone tab once players confirm them.

Money cheats in GTA 6

GTA V had no money cheat, and Rockstar hasn't said anything about one for GTA 6. Any site selling or "generating" GTA 6 money is a scam, and using one can get your account banned.

Before launch, every code list was fake

Cheats aren't announced ahead of time. Players find them in the game. Before November 19 nobody had played GTA 6, so no list from then was real.

  • Never give your PSN or Xbox login to a "cheat" or "money" site.
  • "Generators" for money or items are scams. Cheats are free and need no download.
  • "Early access" or "free copy" offers are fake.
  • A real code is a button sequence or a phone number. If a site wants anything else, close it.
