Why pre-launch lists were fake

Before November 19, 2026 only Rockstar and a handful of reviewers had played GTA 6, and reviewers don't publish cheat lists, so any "full cheat list" posted before then was invented. Many of those pages exist only to collect logins or push downloads.

Will cheats work online?

In GTA V, cheats only worked in Story Mode and never in GTA Online. GTA 6 launched as a single-player game with no online mode on day one.

Is there a money cheat?

GTA V had no money cheat. Anyone selling or "generating" GTA 6 money is running a scam, and using one can get your account banned.
